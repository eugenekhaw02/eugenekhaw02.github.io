# Integration of Thermal Imaging and Facial Recognition in UAV Technology
**Undergraduate Research with National Science and Technology Council (NSTC)** | Feb 2022 – Mar 2025

[<-- Back to Portfolio](README.md)

## Project Overview
Developed a cost-efficient UAV system specifically designed for Search and Rescue (SAR) operations. 

* **Award:** NSTC College Student Research Scholarship (NTD 58,000 grant)
* **Core Tech Stack:** Raspberry Pi 4, FLIR Lepton 3.5 thermal camera, Pi camera, YOLOv8, DeepFace

## Engineering Process
### Hardware Design & Integration
* Designed and integrated a Raspberry Pi 4-based system within a custom 3D-printed housing.
* Seamlessly integrated a FLIR Lepton 3.5 thermal camera and standard Pi camera into the drone's payload.

### AI & Software Development
* Implemented YOLOv8 to achieve real-time human and animal detection from aerial vantage points.
* Integrated DeepFace algorithms to enable accurate facial recognition capabilities during SAR operations.

## Outcome
Successfully enhanced expertise in hardware design, software development, and AI-driven SAR technology integration, delivering a functional, cost-effective search and rescue prototype.
